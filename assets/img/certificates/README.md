# Certificates and evidence photographs

Drop image files here with exactly these names and they appear on the results
page by themselves. Anything missing keeps its designed frame, so the page is
never broken by an absent file.

JPEG or PNG, landscape, at least 1000px on the long edge. Photograph or scan the
certificate flat and straight — a scan at an angle reads as an afterthought and
undoes the point of putting it there.

**Before uploading, check each image for anything that should not be public:**
a student's full name, a national identity number, a home address, a signature
you would rather not publish. Cover it or crop it out first. These files sit in
a public repository.
